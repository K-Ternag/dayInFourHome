This policy explains how the Day in Four app and its website handle personal information. Optional Google sign-in, Plus payments, and Google Drive backups apply when these features are offered in the released version and you choose to use them.

2. Sources and optional permissions

Photos come from items you select in the system photo picker or capture with the camera. Optional notification permission is used for diary reminders. The current app does not request contacts, location, or microphone permissions. Google sign-in and Drive connections require separate consent.

3. External services and data processing

Google handles sign-in, Google Play payments, and Drive backups you connect. Supabase supports server processing for Plus authentication and subscription verification. Diary entries and photos are not sent to the Plus authentication or payment server. If you share images or PDFs or store backups with another service, that service’s policies apply.

Processing countries, regions, and international transfer details: To be announced once the operating environment is finalized. Processors, destination countries, data categories, purposes, transfer methods, retention periods, and related procedures must be confirmed before launch.

See the Google privacy policy, Supabase privacy policy, and GitHub privacy statement.

4. Retention and deletion

You manage records stored on your device. Deleting an entry may leave trash or recovery data. To remove all app data on a device, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Separately saved photos, PDFs, backup files, and Drive data are not removed by this action.

Retention of account, payment, and support records and residual data after deletion: The operating policy is not yet final. Retention periods for each data category, deletion timing, server backup removal, and the basis, scope, and duration of any legally required retention will be specified in the final policy.

Drive backup is designed to retain up to three recent snapshots per device. Older photo fragments may remain, so replacing a snapshot does not necessarily completely delete all original data. See the deletion guide for removing Drive app data.

5. Access, correction, deletion, and withdrawal of consent

You can view and edit on-device diary entries in the app. Use the privacy contact above to request access to, correction or deletion of, or restrictions on processing account information and support records. Limited identity verification may be needed. See account and data deletion for instructions by data type.

6. Safeguards

Account, payment, and Drive communications use HTTPS. Authentication sessions and purchase tokens are protected according to their storage mechanisms. Diary data is managed in app-specific storage. Exported backups, PDFs, and images may contain personal records, so choose where you share them carefully.

7. Advertising, analytics, and this website

The current app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. This website does not use advertising scripts, visitor analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process connection information, such as IP addresses, when hosting the site on GitHub Pages. Text entered in the deletion request form is not sent to or stored on this site’s server. It is processed by your email service and the recipient only if you actually send it from your email app.

8. Policy changes

Material changes to data categories, purposes, or external services will be announced on this page and, where needed, in the app. Any necessary consent procedures will be followed. Refer to the version with a confirmed operator and effective date.
